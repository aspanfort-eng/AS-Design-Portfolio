## About the Project

A custom, responsive portfolio created from scratch to archive and showcase academic, competition, and experimental design projects outside of social media platforms.

### Featured Projects
* **Post-Data** — Foldable travel guide & postcard series
* **Hush** — Noise-monitoring desktop lamp prototype
* **Ashtray** — Editorial magazine layout & social investigation
* **The Dystopia Series** — Book cover collection (*Fahrenheit 451*, *Aniara*, *R.U.R.*)
* **UB Folder** — *Metamorphosis and Vestiges* competition finalist design
* **Festa Major de Sant Pere** — Winning poster design 2024
* **Interrail Travel Guide** — Modular printable travel guides
* **The Archive** — Standardized editorial designers index
* **Experiments** — Cross-disciplinary physical & visual explorations
